How to measure

Use a relaxed stance and a level tape without compressing the skin. Measure height without shoes and neck just below the larynx. For the male equation, measure the abdomen at the navel. For the female equation, measure the natural waist at its narrowest point and hips at the widest point. Use centimeters throughout.

These equations use sex-specific coefficients, not gender identity, and may be less suitable during hormone therapy or for some body types. Tape measurements provide an estimate, not a clinical body-composition assessment.

Calculation methods & sources

Resting energy uses Mifflin–St Jeor: 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age, plus 5 for the male equation or minus 161 for the female equation. Activity multipliers range from 1.2 to 1.9. Body fat uses historical Navy circumference equations with measurements converted to inches.

Macro allocations are planning choices, not measured requirements: protein is 1.6 g/kg for maintenance and 2.0 g/kg for fat loss. Fat supplies 30% of calories; carbs use the remainder (4 kcal/g for protein and carbs, 9 kcal/g for fat).

Fat-loss plans below 1,200 kcal/day (female equation) or 1,500 (male equation), over 1% body weight/week, or for BMI below 18.5 are withheld as conservative screening choices. Personal needs may be higher.
